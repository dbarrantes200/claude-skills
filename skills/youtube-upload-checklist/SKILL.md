# YouTube Upload Checklist

## Set up the idea and packaging

- Identify the channel, format, audience, and one main idea before preparing or reviewing an upload.

- Keep each channel focused on one audience and flag any idea that mixes audiences or changes the channel promise.

- Require a working title and thumbnail concept before production and send the idea back for more work when it cannot be expressed as a clear viewer promise.

- Make the title a curiosity led promise rather than a description of the contents and keep the promise honest enough for the video to deliver it.

- Make the thumbnail complement the title instead of repeating it and use one strong visual with no more than four words.

- Check the thumbnail at phone size and simplify anything that cannot be read at a glance.

- Draw ideas from audience comments, repeated questions, proven formats in the niche, and a maintained idea bank.

- Prequalify each idea by whether it attracts new viewers, serves current viewers, or answers a repeated question.

- Build each video around one useful idea and give the viewer one concrete action to use after watching.

## Protect retention

- Open the first 30 seconds with the viewer problem, the promised result, and the path through the video.

- Deliver the first promise early and avoid opening with a creator introduction.

- Give each minute a reason to continue through a turn, surprise, story, or practical payoff.

- Explain a technical term in plain language as soon as it appears.

- Save closing language for the actual close because early summary language tells viewers the useful part is over.

- Close by opening a related need and sending the viewer to one specific next video or playlist.

- Read the script aloud before recording and cut lines that sound written instead of spoken.

## Apply the house production rules

- Use English for voice and dialogue and Spanish for subtitles or a secondary version unless the user chooses another release pattern for a specific video.

- Trim the last 2 seconds from a NotebookLM export to remove the Gemini outro.

- Watch the final export from start to finish and check clean audio, the intended aspect ratio, the caption source, and the ending.

- Rename the exported file with title keywords before upload.

- Give a Short a strong first 2 seconds, a clear visible promise, captions, and a natural loop because chapters and end screens do not drive Shorts.

## Set the audience correctly

- Set TipsPet, TipsBici, PortalBebé, and YourTipsFitness as not made for kids.

- Set Mila y Leo as made for kids because the channel carries real child directed content and the setting is a legal requirement rather than a growth tactic.

- Expect comments, cards, and end screens to be unavailable or limited on made for kids content and use an in video close that does not depend on those features.

- Recheck current YouTube feature limits before promising a tactic that depends on comments, cards, end screens, notifications, or personalized ads.

## Complete the Studio upload

- Give Studio procedures as clean English steps when the user or a helper will follow them in the app and keep surrounding explanation in Spanish unless the user asks otherwise.

- Confirm the correct channel before starting the upload.

- Paste the prepared title and avoid improvising a new title during upload.

- Put the main link first in the description and follow it with the viewer outcome, a short natural summary, chapters beginning at 0:00, playlist and website links, and three to five hashtags at the end.

- Keep outbound links focused on the user site or offer and avoid sending viewers to other social platforms in the middle of the viewing journey.

- Upload the custom thumbnail or use up to three variants in Test and Compare while changing one variable at a time and letting the test run.

- Add the video to the relevant playlists before publishing and create the series playlist first when a new series needs one.

- Upload or review English captions and add the Spanish subtitle track before publishing.

- Add cards only where the video names another video and only when the feature is available for the selected audience setting.

- Add an end screen in the final 20 seconds of an eligible long video and point it to the next logical video or playlist plus a subscribe element.

- Schedule the video in the regular publishing slot or publish it immediately and reserve Premiere for a launch the user will promote and attend.

- Run one final check of the preview, thumbnail at phone size, playlist, captions, cards, end screen, audience setting, and visibility before publishing.

## Verify and learn after publishing

- Open the public video in a private browsing window and verify playback, title, thumbnail, chapters, captions, playlist placement, and the end screen when present.

- Pin a comment with a question and the user website link when comments are available.

- Reply to early comments during the first hours when comments are available.

- Cross post a Short to TikTok and Instagram Reels when that distribution is part of the approved release plan.

- Log the date, channel, title, format, playlist, and public link in the user tracker when one exists for the project.

- Review click through rate and average view duration after 48 to 72 hours and name one specific improvement for the next video.

- Identify why a successful video worked before repeating the format and separate a packaging failure from a content failure when a video underperforms.

- Report blockers first when reviewing an upload package and mark every item as ready, missing, or not applicable.

## Prepare the deliverable

- Return a channel specific checklist with blockers first, required fixes second, and optional improvements last.

- Include ready to paste title options, a description, chapters, and a pinned comment when the user asks for an upload package.

- Keep a delegation version clean and self contained so a helper can complete the upload without extra explanation.

## Alternatives for better results

- Choose the strongest thumbnail without a formal test when traffic is too low for a meaningful comparison and revisit the choice after more data arrives.

- Improve the title or thumbnail before blaming the topic or the algorithm when the content delivers its promise but few viewers click.
